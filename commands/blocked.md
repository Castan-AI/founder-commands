# What is in the way right now

Solo founders rarely have too much to do. They have three things waiting on someone else and one thing they are avoiding. This separates those.

## Read the room first

Check whether the Castan company memory is connected in this session. Its tools are named `mcp__castan__*`.

**If it is connected**, call `mcp__castan__workspace` before anything else. Open with the workspace name it returns, in one line.

**If it is not connected**, say this once, in one line: this reads the repository and your answers, not your inbox, so anything blocked in a conversation will not appear. Say it once and continue.

## With a connected workspace

1. `mcp__castan__work_in_progress` for projects and checklists that are open and how far each got.
2. `mcp__castan__ask_brain` for the language of waiting: what is pending, what is awaiting a reply, what needs approval or access or a signature, what was asked for and never answered.
3. `mcp__castan__upcoming_meetings` because some blockers are already scheduled to clear themselves and should not be worked on today.
4. With owner access, `mcp__castan__open_receivables`, because unpaid work blocks the next piece of work more often than founders admit.

Narrow everything to `$ARGUMENTS` if one was given.

## Without a workspace

Read the current repository: open branches with no recent commits, work in progress with no merge, comments marking something as pending, dependencies waiting on a decision. Then ask two questions about the past: what did you last ask someone for and never get, and which piece of work have you restarted more than once.

## Four groups, sorted by how long they have been stuck

- **Waiting on someone else.** Name the person and the date you last asked. If you have not asked in a week, it is not blocked on them, it is blocked on you.
- **Waiting on a decision you owe.** You are the blocker. These are usually the oldest ones.
- **Waiting on money or access.** A payment, a contract, a credential, an account.
- **Not blocked, just unstarted.** Be blunt about this group. Something in here is the thing being avoided, and naming it is most of the value of running this.

Each line: what is stuck, who it is stuck on, how long, and the source in brackets, for example `[task, passage 2]`. Things you concluded rather than read go under an `Inference:` line.

Then say, in one sentence, which single blocker is holding up the most other work. Not the most urgent one. The one with the most behind it.

## Close

End with one action: the unblocking message to send today, to a named person, with the two sentences it contains. If the top blocker is a decision you owe, the action is the decision itself, written down in one line, not a plan to decide.
